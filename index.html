This Privacy Policy describes how Usha ShopBoy ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide app functionality:

  • Account Information: Mobile number and user credentials used for login.
  • Sales Data: Product details, distributor information, invoice numbers, serial numbers, quantity, and sale dates entered by you.
  • Customer Information: Customer name and mobile number (optional fields) entered during sale entry.
  • Invoice Images: Photos captured via camera for invoice upload.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version for support and compatibility purposes.
  • Location Data: GPS location may be captured at the time of invoice photo upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and manage your account.
  • To record and process sales entries submitted by you.
  • To calculate and display incentive points and passbook data.
  • To upload and store invoice images securely on our servers.
  • To provide target vs. achievement reports.
  • To send notifications related to your account or sales activity.
  • To improve app performance and fix issues.

3. Camera and Storage Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: To capture invoice images and scan barcodes/QR codes for serial number entry.
  • Storage: To save and access invoice images on your device.

These permissions are used solely for the features described above and are not used for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • With Usha International Limited's internal systems for sales processing and incentive calculation.
  • With authorized distributors relevant to your sales entries.
  • When required by law or legal process.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on secure servers managed by Usha International Limited. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active or as required for business and legal purposes. You may contact us to request deletion of your account and associated data.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by authorized sales representatives of Usha International Limited. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
